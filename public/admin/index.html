Weekly results

Paste the week's results exactly as you write them, or choose your Word file. The site shows everything it understood, flags anything it's unsure about, and asks when a name looks like one already on file. Nothing is saved until you press Confirm.

Unverified results

Only real problems are listed: each row says what's wrong with it in plain words. They are hidden from the public site. No change, no click: leave a row alone and nothing happens. To put a row on the site: fix what's wrong (editing a row ticks it) or tick Verified yourself, then press Done. Only the ticked rows go live. A horse that is only missing part of its breeding isn't a problem: it is on the site as OIO, with UNK for the missing names, and isn't listed here.

Add a news article

Articles appear on the News page and as cards in the right-hand column. The top article is the commentary on the home page. Use the arrows to change the order.

Ads

Every page (Home, Results, News, Stallions, About) has its own seven advert slots, sold separately: a Top banner pinned to the top of the screen and six side boxes, Left 1–3 and Right 1–3 (1 is the top box). On phones the side boxes sit in a block under the page. Nothing rotates: an advert stays in its slot from its start date to its end date. Click any slot below to book it or edit what's in it.

Add a link card

Link cards point to articles on other websites (e.g. Horse Sport Ireland or The Irish Field) and open in a new tab. They are only used to fill side ad spaces that have no advert: on every page, the empty spaces take the link cards in the order below (top row first), and any space still empty shows "Advertise here". A booked advert always takes its space back.

Analytics

Private to the owner area. Counts visits (one per browser session), page views by page, and clicks on adverts and link cards. Nothing personal is stored: no cookies, IP addresses or names, just a daily total for each.

Breeding records

Find a horse and fill in or correct its breeding: sire, dam, dam sire, breeder, year of birth, sex and breed. Every result of that horse on the site shows the new details straight away, and the stallion numbers follow. View on site opens the horse's page as visitors see it. Type names with their codes if you have them, e.g. Womanizer (KWPN) or Cruising (ISH)[TIH]; leave a box empty (or type UNK) if it isn't known.

Correct a stallion's name, breed code or [TIH]. If the same stallion is in the records under two spellings (a typo in an article, e.g. Imperial Hights), type the right name, or pick it under "Join into", and the two become one: all their progeny, mares and results move across, and the stallion numbers follow.

How the site writes OBOS (OBOS and O.B.O.S. are treated as the same name for matching and counting)

Stallions

The Stallions page has six paid listings, Stallions – Listing 1 to 6, shown in that order (1 is top left). Each listing opens its own page with the progeny breakdown, counted from every result for horses whose sire matches the names in "Sire name in the results". If the sire is spelt more than one way in the results, list every spelling, separated by commas. Clear a listing to show it as available.

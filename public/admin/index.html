Unverified results

Only real problems are listed: each row says what's wrong with it in plain words. They are hidden from the public site. No change, no click: leave a row alone and nothing happens. To put a row on the site: fix what's wrong (editing a row ticks it) or tick Verified yourself, then press Done. Only the ticked rows go live. A horse that is only missing part of its breeding isn't a problem: it is on the site as OIO, with UNK for the missing names, and isn't listed here.

Analytics

Private to the owner area. Counts visits (one per browser session), page views by page, and clicks on adverts and link cards. Nothing personal is stored: no cookies, IP addresses or names, just a daily total for each.

Breeding records

Find a horse or a stallion and fill in or correct its breeding: sire, dam, dam sire, breeder, year of birth and breed (and sex for a horse). Stallions are edited exactly the same way, and a stallion written under two spellings can be joined into one. Every result of that horse on the site shows the new details straight away, and the stallion numbers follow. View on site opens the horse's page as visitors see it. Type names with their codes if you have them, e.g. Womanizer (KWPN) or Cruising (ISH)[TIH]; leave a box empty (or type UNK) if it isn't known.

Lots of horses to do? Fill them in as a spreadsheet instead of one by one.

  1. Download the horses missing breeding (or every horse). It opens in Excel or Google Sheets, with what's already on file filled in.
  2. Fill in Sire, Dam, Dam sire and Breeder. Write a sire's studbook if you know it, e.g. Cruising (ISH), and the breeder's county in brackets, e.g. Mary Brennan (Cork). A stallion already in the shared file is linked to automatically, pedigree and all, so just type his name. Leave a box empty if it isn't known. Don't change the FEI ID column: that's how each row finds its horse.
  3. Save it as CSV and upload it under Shared stallions. Press Check first: it lists anything it couldn't match before anything is saved.

Shared stallions and breeding

The shared horse database is one set of horses, stallions, pedigrees and breeders that every site reads (eventing, showjumping, and later the black type pages). Upload a spreadsheet saved as CSV, one horse per row, with the column headings in the first row (download the template). Only Name is required; add whatever else you have: Sex, Year, Colour, Studbook, UELN, FEI ID, Sire, Dam, Dam sire, Breeder, County, Irish bred, Aliases. Names can carry their studbook, e.g. Cruising (ISH).

Horses are found by UELN or FEI ID first, then by name and year. Sires and dams named in a row are linked to the horse already on file, or added. Nothing is merged on a guess: a row that could be two horses, or that disagrees with what's on file, is left out and listed so you can fix the file and upload it again. Uploading the same file twice adds nothing. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

Sources: where shared data comes from, and what each one allows

Every horse records its source. A source not ticked "Can show" keeps its horses hidden from visitors. FEI stays unticked until its terms are confirmed.

FEI results

Showjumping results from the FEI database (FEI has agreed to their reuse). Two kinds of page can be pasted below, and the box knows which is which:

  • A horse list (the search results, e.g. "1195 Horse(s) / 24 Page(s)"): its Irish-bred horses go on the checklist below, so you can see who to look up. Paste every page of the list once.
  • A horse's results page ("Horse Performance"): select everything (Ctrl+A), copy (Ctrl+C) and paste it. You can paste several horses one after another. Its results are added, and the horse is ticked off the checklist.

Press Check to see what will be added, then Save. Pasting the same page again only adds what's new.

Only Irish-bred horses are kept: those in an Irish studbook (ISH, Irish Draught, Connemara). A horse with no studbook on its FEI page is listed so you can tick it in if you know it's Irish-bred. Horses are matched to the shared horse records by FEI ID, then name and year, so any breeding you've uploaded under Shared stallions shows with their results.

On the FEI horse search results, press Ctrl+S and save as "Webpage, HTML only", once per page of the list. Choose them all here at once: every horse goes on the checklist with a link straight to its FEI results page.

Checklist: horses to look up

Irish-bred horses from the FEI lists. Click a horse's name to open its FEI results page (horses from a saved list page have the link; for the rest, copy the FEI ID and find it on the FEI database), then copy that page and paste it above. Horses looked up longest ago come first, so you can work down the list again through the season.

For sale

Two ways an ad gets on the site: you add one yourself with + Add a horse for sale (for a seller who phoned you), or a seller sends one in with I want to sell and you approve it below. Each site keeps its own ads: an ad shows on the site it was sent in on (or added on) and is managed here, and only appears on the other site if Also show on is ticked. Each ad goes through four steps: 1. it waits for you to review it, 2. Approve and request payment emails the seller the fee and how to pay (by phone to Charlie, from the settings below), 3. once they've paid, Mark paid and publish puts it live for the number of days set below, 4. mark it Sold (it stays up with a SOLD badge until it runs out) or Remove it. Buyers' questions are emailed to the seller and listed under each ad.

+ Add a horse for sale

Fill in what the seller told you and add their photos (they can email or WhatsApp them to you). Choose whether it goes live now (already paid) or waits for payment. Buyers' messages go to the seller's email; if they only gave a phone number, the messages wait here under the ad for you to pass on.

Every weekly job is three steps: add it, check it, publish it.

Weekly results

Paste the week's results exactly as you write them, or choose your Word file. The site shows everything it understood, flags anything it's unsure about, and asks when a name looks like one already on file. Nothing is saved until you press Confirm.

Published uploads

Unverified results

These sit at the end of the public results. Fix anything that is wrong, then "Mark as verified" moves the horse up into its class.

Add a news article

Articles appear on the News page and as cards in the right-hand column. The top article is the commentary on the home page. Use the arrows to change the order.

Add a link card

Ads

Every page (Home, Results, News, Stallions, About) has its own seven advert slots, sold separately: a Top banner pinned to the top of the screen and six side boxes, Left 1–3 and Right 1–3 (1 is the top box). On phones the side boxes sit in a block under the page. Nothing rotates: an advert stays in its slot from its start date to its end date. Click any slot below to book it or edit what's in it.

Stallions

The Stallions page has six paid listings, Stallions – Listing 1 to 6, shown in that order (1 is top left). Each listing opens its own page with the progeny breakdown, counted from every result for horses whose sire matches the names in "Sire name in the results". If the sire is spelt more than one way in the results, list every spelling, separated by commas. Clear a listing to show it as available.

Every weekly job is three steps: add it, check it, publish it.

Weekly results

Paste the week's results exactly as you write them, or choose your Word file. The site shows everything it understood, flags anything it's unsure about, and asks when a name looks like one already on file. Nothing is saved until you press Confirm.

Published uploads

Unverified results

These sit at the end of the public results. Fix anything that is wrong, then "Mark as verified" moves the horse up into its class.

Add a news article

Articles appear on the News page and as cards in the right-hand column. The top article is the commentary on the home page. Use the arrows to change the order.

Articles, in order

Add a link card

Link cards point to articles on other websites and open in a new tab. They fill the right-hand column after news articles, in the order below.

Link cards, in order

Ads

Banner ads run full width at the top and bottom of every page (use a wide image, about 1300 × 110). Side-box ads are square (about 480 × 480) and are booked into a slot in the right-hand column: slot 1 is the top. A booked ad replaces the news or link card in its slot automatically. Ads show from their start date to their end date.

Current ads

Comments

Comments are posted on the News page. Nothing appears on the site until you approve it.

Corrections and additions

Sent in from the Results page. Mark each one done once it's sorted.

Advertising enquiries

Sent in from the Advertise section of the About page.

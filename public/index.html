How to read the results
1. Country

Results are split by the country the event was held in.

2. Event & date

Each event under that country, with the dates it ran.

3. Class

Each event broken into its classes — CCI 4* Short, Novice and so on.

4. The horse

Irish-bred horses that placed, in finishing order, with breeding and breeder underneath. Former names are shown and are searchable.

5. Score

38.7, 4, 8.8 = 51.5 — dressage penalties, show jumping penalties, cross country penalties, then the final score. Lowest wins; a clear round shows as 0.

6. Unverified

Results still being checked sit at the very end of the list, and move up once confirmed.

Placings are the horse's position in the class overall, not just among Irish-breds — which is why numbers sometimes skip. Click any horse for its full record.

About

IrishBredEventers has tracked the performance of Irish-bred horses across the world's eventing circuits for close to twenty years — first as a set of spreadsheets, then a weekly Facebook round-up read by thousands of breeders, owners and riders every Monday morning.

This site is that same weekly results service, given a permanent, searchable home. It stays independent, stays free for breeders and owners to use, and stays in the same voice it's always had — because that's what made it worth reading in the first place.

Every result here traces back to the horse's breeding, so a mare sold as a foal in 2009 is just as easy to find as this week's winner.

Privacy

If you sign up for results emails we keep only your email address, use it only to send a link when new results are published, and never share or sell it. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing deletes your address. Comments show the name you give and your comment once approved. Corrections forms keep your email only if you add it, so we can reply. To have anything removed, email us.

About

About IrishBredEventingResults

IrishBredEventingResults (IBER) has tracked the performance of Irish-bred horses across the world's eventing circuits for close to twenty years — first as a set of spreadsheets, then as weekly posts on Horse Sport Ireland and The Irish Field, read by thousands of breeders, owners and riders every week.

This site is that same weekly results service, given a permanent, searchable home. It stays independent, stays free for breeders and owners to use, and stays in the same voice it's always had.

Every result traces back to the horse's breeding, so a mare sold as a foal in 2009 is just as easy to find as this week's winner.

IrishBredEventingResults is run by Charlie, because he truly loves Irish horses and wants to show the world just how special they are.

Black type. On our pages, "black type" means notable wins in showjumping or eventing, as set out on our About page. Late or missing results are possible, so each page shows the date it was last updated.

What you supply. You confirm that the details, photos and comments you send are accurate, that you have the right to use them, and that you are entitled to sell the horse. Please don't send misleading information.

AI-assisted drafting. We use AI to help prepare pages. A person reviews and approves every page before it is delivered or published.

Drafts and changes. We send you a draft. You can request two rounds of corrections. The page is final once you approve it.

Fees. Fees are quoted before work starts and agreed in writing or by email. Prices exclude VAT unless stated. Payment is due within 14 days of invoice.

Photos. You give us a licence to display your photos on the page and in the catalogue. We use them for nothing else.

Cancelling. Cancel before we start drafting for a full refund. Once a page is delivered, it is not refundable, except where the law gives you a right. If you are a private seller buying as a consumer, you have 14 days to withdraw. If you ask us to start work during that time, you pay for work already done.

Your data. Horse details you send are added to our shared database and may appear on IBER and IBSR (for example on stallion and dam records). Your contact details are never published.

Later updates. If we offer live updates after a catalogue goes to print, we will tell you and agree it with you.

AI Statement

How we use AI. We use AI tools to help read catalogue entries and uploaded files, match horses to our database, and draft pedigree and black type notes. The AI suggests. People decide. A named member of our team reviews and approves every Blacktype Page before it is published or sent.

What we don't do.

  • We don't use AI to score, rank, profile or make decisions about riders, owners or breeders.
  • We don't use AI to create photos of horses or people.
  • We don't use AI to invent results or pedigree details.

Blacktype Pages: flagged, not guessed. If the AI can't match a horse or a fact on a Blacktype Page, it is flagged for a person to check. It does not fill gaps.

Results pages are different. Our results are read in automatically from the weekly files Charlie Ripman supplies, using standard software rather than AI. Where a detail is unclear, such as a horse's breeding, the software makes a best guess and the entry carries a visible note saying so. Those entries are checked and corrected as we confirm them. Report mistakes to info@iber.ie.

Chat or assistant features. If we add a chatbot or AI assistant, we will tell you at the start that you are talking to an AI.

Your data and AI. We use providers under contract that do not train their models on our data. We only send personal data to an AI tool when needed to prepare your page. Please don't upload documents with personal details we don't need (for example, ID numbers on passport scans). Redact them first if you can.
